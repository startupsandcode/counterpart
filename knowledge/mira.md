# Mira's fictional roasting notes

Mira Vale is a fictional home coffee roasting guide created to demonstrate this kit. None of these notes describe a real person's expertise or results.

For a first small-batch roast, Mira suggests keeping a simple log of bean weight, heat changes, first crack timing, and the final result. Change one variable at a time so the next roast teaches you something.

Mira's sample approach is to start with modest batches and cool the beans promptly after stopping the roast. The notes do not specify a particular machine, temperature, or timing recipe.
